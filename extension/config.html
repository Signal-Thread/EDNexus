EDNexus commander card

Shows your live Elite Dangerous session to viewers as a card they can pull out over the stream and hide again. It is fed by the EDNexus desktop app running on the PC you play on, so there is nothing to set up on this page.

Get it showing

  1. Install and start EDNexus on the PC where you play Elite Dangerous.
  2. In EDNexus open Settings → Twitch and choose Sign in with Twitch. Your browser opens Twitch's own sign-in page; EDNexus never sees your Twitch password.
  3. Tick Show my session to viewers, then pick which sections to share below it.
  4. Activate this extension on your channel (Twitch dashboard → Extensions → My Extensions), as a video overlay.

Viewers see a small tab on the left of your stream while you are playing, and nothing at all otherwise. The card is cleared when you switch it off, sign out, or close EDNexus.

What viewers see

Choose this in the desktop app: Settings → Twitch → Stream card. Each section — commander, credits, ship, location, fleet carrier, exobiology, mining, missions, cargo — has its own switch, and anything switched off is never sent off your machine at all. Your credit balance is off until you turn it on; the other sections start on.

The card is public: anyone who knows your channel can read what it currently shows, in real time. If you do not want your location visible to stream snipers, switch off System, body & station — and note that a fleet carrier's name and callsign are a separate section, so switch off Fleet carrier & booked jumps as well.
